Gym Log is a training ledger for people who log their own lifting. It opens on your body: which muscles are recovered, which are still on the clock, and one tap to start a session.

READINESS FIRST
• A rotatable 3D body map coloured by fatigue, strength, hypertrophy or efficiency
• Per-muscle recovery clocks that scale with how hard you actually trained
• A whole-body readiness gauge before you pick what to train

LOG LIVE, SET BY SET
• Pick any movement from a one-thumb ring picker — muscle groups, exercises and your recent lifts in one dial
• Rotary reps and weight dials, one-tap picks from your last set, last session or the next progression step
• Set stopwatch, rest timer with a lock-screen alert, reps-in-reserve, warm-up ramp and plate calculator
• Personal-record flash the moment you beat a best

KNOW WHAT TO EXPECT TODAY
• A fatigue-adjusted forecast for every exercise: flies after six sets of bench aren't flies done fresh
• See the load to aim for at your usual reps, how much strength today's work has cost, and what caused it
• Progressive-overload nudges: add a rep, or move the load up when you've earned it

UNDERSTAND YOUR TRAINING
• Session history with effort, hydration and protein estimates
• Per-exercise estimated 1RM, trend chart and reps you can expect at the loads you train
• Export sessions as PNG or PDF, or share a Markdown recap to an AI assistant of your choice for analysis

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no sign-in, no ads
• Your training data stays on your phone — nothing is uploaded
• Works fully offline; back up and restore everything to a single file whenever you like

Available in English and Romanian.

Gym Log is a training log, not medical advice. Check with a professional before starting a new training programme.
