Gym Log · Training Ledger

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Gym Log ("the app") is a privacy-first, offline-first training log, available on the web and as an Android app. The short version: your training data stays on your device. There are no accounts, no logins, no ads, and no server that stores your workouts.

Data you create

Your training sessions, the sets you log, personal records, tested one-rep maxes, the body weight you enter, and your preferences (language, theme, rest-alert timing) are stored only on your device — in the browser's local storage, or in the Android app's private storage. We never receive, transmit, or store this data. Clearing your browser data or uninstalling the app removes it.

Body weight is optional and is used only on your device, to personalise the protein and calorie estimates.

Backups

You can export everything to a single backup file and restore it later; the file is created on your device and goes wherever you choose to save or send it. On Android, the operating system's own device backup (to your Google account, if you have it turned on) may include the app's data, under Google's terms — the app itself sends nothing.

Sharing is initiated by you

When you export or share a session recap (PNG, PDF, JSON, XML or Markdown), the content is produced on your device and handed to whatever destination you choose: your phone's share sheet, a file download, or the clipboard.

"Analyze with Claude"

This feature does not call any AI service on your behalf. It prepares a text summary of the sessions you select and hands it to an assistant you open yourself (by sharing or copying it). Anything you then send is governed by that service's own privacy policy.

Android permissions

  • Notifications and alarms & reminders (exact alarms) — used only for the rest-timer alert, so it can sound when your rest is over even with the phone locked. Both are requested when you first turn the alert on, and declining them only disables or delays that alert.
  • Internet — used only to load the app's typefaces (see below). The app works fully offline.

Typefaces

The app's fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. As with any web request, Google receives standard technical information such as your IP address; no training data or identifiers from the app are included. See Google's privacy policy for how it handles these requests.

Anonymous usage analytics

The Android app sends no analytics. The hosted web version may use privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics (for example, Plausible) to count anonymous, aggregate usage — such as how many people opened the app or used a feature. When enabled, it:

  • uses no cookies and sets no device or user identifier;
  • collects no personal data and none of your training data;
  • honours your browser's Do Not Track / Global Privacy Control signal.

Self-hosted builds send nothing at all.

Health information

Workout logs and body weight can be considered health-related information. Because they never leave your device through the app, we do not collect, sell, or share them.

Children

The app is a general fitness tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves; material changes will be reflected by the date above.
