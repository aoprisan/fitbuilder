Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 May 2026

Gym Log ("the app") is a privacy-first, offline-first training tool. The short version: your training data stays on your device. There are no accounts, no logins, and no server that stores your routines or workout logs.

Data you create

Routines, training sessions, your trainer name, brand logo, and preferences are stored only in your browser's local storage (or, in the mobile app, on the device). We never receive, transmit, or store this data. Clearing your browser data or uninstalling the app removes it.

Sharing is initiated by you

When you export or share a routine sheet, session recap, or share link, the content is produced on your device and handed to whatever destination you choose (your OS share sheet, a file download, the clipboard, or an AI assistant you paste into). Share links encode the routine directly in the link — no copy is kept by us.

AI ("Build with Claude") features

The Claude features do not call any AI service on your behalf. They prepare a text prompt and hand it to an assistant you open yourself; anything you then send is governed by that service's own privacy policy.

Anonymous usage analytics

Hosted versions of the app may use privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics (for example, Plausible) to count anonymous, aggregate usage — such as how many people opened the app or used a feature. This is configured per deployment and, when enabled:

  • uses no cookies and sets no device or user identifier;
  • collects no personal data and none of your training data;
  • never includes share-link contents (routine payloads are stripped before any ping);
  • honours your browser's Do Not Track / Global Privacy Control signal.

Self-hosted and offline builds send nothing at all.

Children

The app is a general fitness tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves; material changes will be reflected by the date above.
